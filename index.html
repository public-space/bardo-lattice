How the array computes

Size
One cell for every five minutes of the current year: days × 24 hours × 12 slots. The dimensions come from the clock, leap years included, and the days before your first instance are in the array whether or not you lived them here.
Seal
Every five minutes the open cell closes. Your next keystroke after a word ends starts a new block in the new cell, and a light timestamp appears the way a chat thread marks time.
Instance · rebirth
Each opening of the program is a new instance. Every cell records which instances passed through it.
Pause · Lynch
The longest silence between keystrokes inside one cell. Duration is measured, not skipped.
Excision · Cronenberg
Every character you delete is counted against the cell whose text you cut. The body of the text keeps its scars.
Edits from later · Dick
Revising a sealed block writes into a cell whose time has already ended. The array records it, and the cell turns cold blue. Time here runs both ways.
Cut-up · Burroughs & Gysin
Text from the array, cut into short runs and reshuffled, so the lines say what the writer did not plan to.
Extinguish · nirvāṇa, "blowing out"
Removes a cell's text and keeps its trace. The cell stays marked, and empty. It cannot be undone.
